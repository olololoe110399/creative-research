# Investigation method

Begin with a decision, not “find the best format.” Record operator, accounts,
publication/capture windows, content type and the available population. A scraped
sample may omit deleted/private posts or stop at a collection limit.

Build a small hypothesis ledger: claim, observable prediction, rival explanation,
required sources, counterexample search and disposition (supported / mixed /
unsupported / unknown). Test at least one rival for every strategic inference.

Move from performance candidates to their creative analysis, family membership,
cadence and source posts. Inspect unsuccessful examples of the same format and
successful examples of other formats. Don't use a top-only selection as the cohort.
Avoid assuming repeated posts, family members and linked pattern rows are independent.

If data is missing, name the missing table/column/time window and the smallest
additional observation that would change the decision. No automatic provider calls.
One occurrence is an example, not a recurring pattern. Conflicting metrics can
reflect different objectives: expose the tradeoff instead of inventing an average.

Copy measured numbers from engine tables. Clearly label human/agent interpretation,
model-extracted descriptions and proposed experiments. Never infer private intent,
sales, profitability or causal impact from public engagement alone.

Before delivery: check source identity, data age, cohort comparability, denominator,
counterexamples, sample dependence, rights, unknowns and testability of next actions.
Confidence is a qualitative judgment with reasons, not a calibrated probability.
