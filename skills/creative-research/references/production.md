# Original production planning

Translate evidence-linked findings into original ideas, not copied source assets.
Use the production brief template: audience/problem, original hook/structure,
execution, finding links, rights review, measurement plan and approval checkpoints.

Distinguish a reusable structural observation (e.g. problem → demonstration → CTA)
from third-party expression. Public availability does not grant reuse rights.
Keep assets original or explicitly licensed; claims, testimonials, music, trademarks
and product footage need their own clearance. Don't fabricate rights attestations.

A 30-day plan should name proposed slots, concept/variant, hypothesis/finding IDs,
intended signal and review cadence. Explain why it tests the evidence, where it
explores unknowns and what would stop/adjust it. Proposed quantities are planning
choices, not source metrics or promised results.

Keep drafts in chat or new agent-reports/ files. Do not edit
`data/07_knowledge/operating_state/`, generate official team approvals or post to
TikTok. Existing Lab/production handoff remains unchanged and is a separate workflow.
