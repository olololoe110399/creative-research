# Creative family investigation

Read `data/06_analytics/creative_families.parquet` with
`creative_family_members.parquet`; follow member `post_uid` to
`data/05_master/creative_analysis.parquet` and `creative_sequence.parquet`.
Families are deterministic similarity candidates, not established strategic intent.

Compare hooks, sequence roles, topic/angle, format and product placement across
multiple members. Find visually/semantically different members and similar posts
outside the family. Separate exact repetition from an adaptable structural pattern.
Do not replace clustering, reassign membership or silently rerun paid judgments.

Existing cached/model judgments are evidence with model limitations, not ground
truth. A one-member family is an isolated creative example. Repeated copies across
accounts can share a source and are not independent proof that a tactic works.

For each useful family report: exact family/member IDs, observed common structure,
variations, counterexamples, missing detail and a proposed original adaptation.
Use structural inspiration; don't copy hooks, footage, visual identity or licensed assets.
